## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Do I need a laptop to learn?**
A: Yes, we highly recommend a laptop for writing code and building projects. However, our platform is mobile-friendly for reading lessons and answering quizzes!

**Q: Do you offer certificates?**
A: Yes! Upon completing a course track, you receive a verified Digital Era certificate that you can showcase on LinkedIn or your resume.

**Q: I don't have internet all the time. Can I still use the app?**
A: Absolutely! Digital Era has an "Offline-First" mode. If you log in and visit the Dashboard while connected to WiFi, you can click "Offline Downloads" to download full courses to your browser. You can then disconnect and keep reading lessons, taking quizzes, and even running Python code offline. Progress will sync when you get back online.

**Q: My internet data is expensive. Does the app use a lot of data?**
A: We've got you covered. You can enable "Data Saver Mode" from the top navigation bar. This turns off heavy animations, stops background translations, and waits to download the Python engine until you actually need it, saving up to 80% of your data per session!

**Q: Can I get help if I am stuck on a code problem?**
A: Yes, we have a built-in AI Tutor ("Study Buddy") right inside the Workspace. You can ask it to debug your code or explain concepts. If you're offline, the AI even has a cached brain that can still answer common errors!

**Q: Do you offer physical classes?**
A: Yes, we offer physical, hands-on training at our center in Ikorodu, Lagos. Please call or WhatsApp us to schedule a visit or enroll in physical cohorts.
